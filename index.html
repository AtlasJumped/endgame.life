Works todayThe setup rating, the connected areas, and the next connectors to add are real parts of the downloadable claudebomb.com dashboard file. It scores 1 to 100 from what your assistant can actually read.

The vision

Our picture of about 2036

Our vision, not fact. Nothing on this page is a forecast or a promise. It is where we think this could go.

A microphone button that never goes away

In the bottom left corner of every phone, always, is a microphone button. Press it and talk. It goes straight to your own AI agent.

Your agent learns you, in your own database

It records what you say and learns about you. The record is stored in a database that belongs to you, not to an app company.

Danger assets stay behind a physical key

Money is the main danger asset. We picture a hardware key that you carry, or keep in a lockbox at home, with a manual switch. Nothing that moves money works until you flip it. Try the switch.

Money is locked. No agent can move it.

Youvoice, habits, places match
Fakedoes not match

Oversharing with the right ally protects you

Our idea is that if your agent holds your voice and your activity, it can tell the real you from a fake, so a stolen ID matters much less once money is locked. Whether this holds up against fast-improving fakes is unknown. It needs testing.

Fewer screens, more faces

A watch or a bracelet does what a phone does, so more of us leave the phone at home. We hope the time saved goes to looking at each other.

Why this is urgent

Phone AI may soon be controlled by a few companies. If that happens before an open picture exists, the open-source community loses its grip. We want to share this vision first, and build it together as an open-source community.

An honest note

This is a vision, not a promise.

The people are made up. The screens are storyboards, not screenshots. Here is where things stand.

Real today

  • A free dashboard file at claudebomb.com that you give to an AI assistant to set up.
  • A setup rating from 1 to 100, based on what the assistant can read.
  • Tabs for today, agenda, inbox, messages, money, fun, family and friends, work, and you.
  • Rules: you are in charge, nothing leaves, read-only money.

Hoped for

  • One tap to connect most services.
  • Standard read-only access at banks and apps.
  • Drafts that sound like you.
  • Shared plans among people who opt in.
  • Employers and people agreeing on a privacy firewall.

Unsolved

  • How to share dashboards safely when they touch work data.
  • Security risks, including malicious files and tricked assistants.
  • Whether assistants stay accurate enough to trust with drafts.
  • Whether companies will allow it.
